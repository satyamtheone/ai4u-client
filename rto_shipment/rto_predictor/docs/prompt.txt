create e2e machine learning / AI pipeline(in python) for Shipment RTO(Return to Origin) Predictor. Basically this pipeline will take input as Order/Shipment details and predict if this order will delivered or not to end customer. 

Functionality/Task
1. Simple function read csv of new orders.
1.1 Feature Impact on prediction 
CLIENT_NAME(Very Low), 
CUSTOMER_NAME(Low), 
CUSTOMER_PHONE(Very High), CUSTOMER_ADDRESS(Very High), 
CUSTOMER_CITY(Low or based on previous history), 
CUSTOMER_STATE(Low or based on previous history),
PINCODE(Low or based on previous history),
PAYMENT_MODE(High)
COURIER_NAME(High)
ITEM_DESCRIPTION(High or based on previous history)
WEIGHT(Low)
QTY	(Low)
INVOICE_VALUE(Low)

1.2 Predition based on CLIENT_NAME, CUSTOMER_NAME, CUSTOMER_PHONE, CUSTOMER_ADDRESS, CUSTOMER_CITY, CUSTOMER_STATE, PINCODE, PAYMENT_MODE, COURIER_NAME, ITEM_DESCRIPTION
2. Feed to ML pipeline predict the outcome.
3. Find the reason why it will delivered and why it will not delivered based on Step 2 outcome.
4. Recommendation step
5. Find the top 5 similar kind of orders/shipment details from the history data. 
6. Retrain the base model with updated data once in day and upload ml/ai snapshot to disk with folder structure <yyyy_mm_dd>
7. Use new retrained data for future prediction.
8. Generate Summary of over all outcome Perfomance of COURIER_NAME in past, DELIVERY chances in this area of Pincode etc.
